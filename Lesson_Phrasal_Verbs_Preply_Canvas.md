## Block 2: The Phrasal Verb Decoder (Group by PARTICLE, Not Verb!)
```text
🧠 THE SECRET FOR SPANISH SPEAKERS:
• Formal verbs in English = Latin (deploy, resolve, postpone, eliminate).
• Everyday meeting English = Phrasal Verbs (Verb + Particle).
• Don't memorize by the verb—read the PARTICLE + picture the physical action!

1️⃣ [ OUT ] = Expand outward OR Eliminate a problem completely
• ROLL OUT     = deploy gradually (unroll a carpet across a room)
• IRON OUT     = fix small bugs/issues (iron wrinkles out of a shirt)
• RULE OUT     = eliminate a cause ("We ruled out a network issue")
• PHASE OUT    = gradually retire legacy code/systems

2️⃣ [ UP ] = Make visible / Appear OR Increase
• FLAG UP      = raise a risk/warning early (wave a red flag)
• BRING UP     = raise a topic in a meeting
• SPIN UP      = launch a server/environment quickly
• SCALE UP     = increase capacity

3️⃣ [ OFF ] = Launch / Separate / Pass a checkpoint
• KICK OFF     = start a meeting or project (like a football match)
• SIGN OFF ON  = officially approve (sign & send off your desk)
• PUT OFF      = postpone / delay to later ("Don't put it off")

4️⃣ [ DOWN ] = Reduce / Split into pieces / Stop
• BREAK DOWN   = split a big project into small tickets
• NARROW DOWN  = reduce 10 bug theories down to 1 or 2
• TURN DOWN    = reject a proposal or offer
```

## Block 3: Rapid Decode + "The Pronoun Sandwich"
```text
⚡ PART A: UPGRADE TO NATURAL TECH ENGLISH
Replace the [BRACKETED] formal verb with a phrasal verb from Block 2:

1. "Let's [POSTPONE] the migration until Monday."
2. "We [ELIMINATED] a database bottleneck during debugging."
3. "Can you [LAUNCH/START] a staging environment for QA?"
4. "Let's [SPLIT] this epic into smaller sprint tickets."
5. "We [REDUCED] the root cause to the billing microservice."
6. "Wait until the Tech Lead [APPROVES] the release."

🥪 PART B: THE PRONOUN SANDWICH RULE
When using IT or THEM, put the pronoun IN THE MIDDLE:
✅ "Roll out the fix"  =  "Roll IT out"     (❌ NOT "Roll out it")
✅ "Iron out the bugs" =  "Iron THEM out"   (❌ NOT "Iron out them")
✅ "Put off the call"  =  "Put IT off"      (❌ NOT "Put off it")

⚠️ Exception (3-word verbs stay together):
✅ "Sign off on IT" (3 words = pronoun at the end!)
```

## Block 4: Live Role-Play — Amsterdam Billing Outage Post-Mortem
```text
🚨 SCENARIO: AMSTERDAM BILLING OUTAGE POST-MORTEM
A missing dead-letter queue retry caused 45 mins of failed invoices & customer churn risk.

🎯 YOUR CHALLENGE: Deploy at least 5 phrasal verbs live!
[ ] kick off        [ ] flag up / bring up   [ ] rule out
[ ] narrow down     [ ] iron out             [ ] break down
[ ] put off         [ ] sign off on          [ ] roll out (it/them)

🗣️ ROUND 1 — Dutch Engineering Team (Polder Model):
• Bram (Senior Dev) says: "Let's just spin up a hotfix this Saturday and roll it out!"
• Your goals:
  1. Kick off the meeting & open up the floor to Sanne (junior dev).
  2. Explain how we ruled out the DB and narrowed it down to the retry queue.
  3. Push back on working Saturday: explain why we shouldn't sign off on a rushed fix until we iron out the alerts and break down the tasks on Monday.

🗣️ ROUND 2 — Anxious Product Manager (60-Second Plain-English Update):
• PM asks: "I have a board call in 10 minutes! What happened and when is it fixed?"
• Your goal: Reassure the PM in plain English—what we ruled out, what we're ironing out, and how we'll roll it out safely on Monday to protect retention.
```
