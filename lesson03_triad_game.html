LEVEL 1 EXAMPLE SCRIPT

Card: Patch vs. Workaround vs. Overhaul

Louie: "Diego, 90 seconds! Explain the difference between a Patch, a Workaround, and an Overhaul in software systems. Forbidden words: fix, problem, change, new."

Diego: "Okay, a Workaround is a temporary detour when a feature fails—you achieve the goal by using an alternative route without touching the code. A Patch is a targeted software update deployed to repair a specific bug. An Overhaul is far more radical than a patch—you rebuild the entire legacy architecture from scratch."

LEVEL 2 EXAMPLE SCRIPT

Card: Loudness vs. Headroom vs. Clarity

Louie: "I've picked one of the three. Listen to this DJ scenario: 'At a club in Madrid, the opening DJ pushes the channel gain and master fader into the red all night. The bass sounds distorted and flat, and when the headliner comes on at 2 AM, there is zero margin left on the sound system to make the drop hit harder.' Which word did the opening DJ destroy?"

Diego: "He destroyed the Headroom! Because he confused raw Loudness with impact, caused signal clipping, and killed the Clarity."

LEVEL 3 EXAMPLE SCRIPT

Card: Output vs. Deliverable vs. Outcome

Louie: "Assign The Villain, The Trap, and The Ideal in a consulting project."

Diego:
• 🦹 The Villain is Raw Output: "Just writing 10,000 lines of code or working 60 hours with zero strategic direction."
• 🪤 The Trap is The Deliverable: "Teams celebrate just handing over the software on time, even if the client's staff doesn't know how to use it!"
• 🏆 The Ideal is The Business Outcome: "When the deployment actually streamlines their workflow and boosts ROI."
