Prompt 01 • Circumlocution Reflex

Describing Without Naming

Pick three objects on your desk or in your office. Without saying their name, practice explaining their material, physical appearance, and practical function in two flowing sentences.

Prompt 02 • Sentence Stretcher

The 4-Layer Workplace Stretch

Take this 5-word seed: "The client approved the project." Apply our 4-layer scaffolding formula (Adjectives $\rightarrow$ Location $\rightarrow$ Cause with because $\rightarrow$ Time clause with while) to reach 20+ words!

Prompt 03 • Operational Precision

Eliminating the Hesitation Barrier

In your next tech review or operational sync, practice the verbal precision from our bomb disposal drill: use unambiguous terminology, confirm coordinates/numbers explicitly, and anchor answers with reasons ("because...") and contexts ("while we were...").
