🧭 The 4-Particle Decoder

Don't memorize blindly!

In Spanish, formal verbs (deploy, resolve, postpone) are easy because they're Latin. To master English phrasal verbs, look at the particle + picture the action:

📤 OUT = Expand outward OR Eliminate completely
roll out, iron out, rule out, phase out
⬆️ UP = Make visible / Appear OR Increase
flag up, bring up, spin up, scale up
🚀 OFF = Launch, Separate, or Pass a gate
kick off, sign off on, put off, call off
⬇️ DOWN = Reduce, Split into pieces, or Track
break down, narrow down, turn down, pay down
